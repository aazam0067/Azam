Good to Know

Quick Questions,
Straight Answers

Everything you need to know before you message us — no surprises, no fine print.

Is this legal?+

We source and resell digital titles at discounted rates — the same way a discount bookstore or reseller operates, just digital and faster. Files are for personal reading use.

What if you can't find my book?+

In the rare case we can't source a title, you don't pay — simple as that. We'll always tell you upfront before any payment is made.

Is the ~10 minute delivery guaranteed?+

Most orders are delivered within 10 minutes of payment confirmation since titles are typically ready to send immediately. Rare or obscure titles may take a little longer — we'll let you know upfront if so.

Do I need to create an account?+

No accounts, no apps, no signup forms. Just message us on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn, and we'll handle the rest.

Which payment methods do you accept?+

UPI, PayPal, and major debit/credit cards — whichever is most convenient for you, from anywhere in the world.

Will the file work on my device?+

Yes. We deliver in PDF, EPUB, or MOBI — compatible with Kindle, iPad, Kobo, Android, and any e-reader or desktop app. Just tell us your preferred format.
