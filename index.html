          Good to Know

          Quick Questions,
          Straight Answers

          Everything you need to know before you message us — no surprises, no fine print.

          What if you can't find my book?+

          In the rare case we can't source a title, you don't pay — simple as that. We'll always tell you upfront before any payment is made.

          Is the ~10 minute delivery guaranteed?+

          Most orders are delivered within 10 minutes of payment confirmation since titles are typically ready to send immediately. Rare or obscure titles may take a little longer — we'll let you know upfront if so.

          Do I need to create an account?+

          No accounts, no apps, no signup forms. Just message us on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn, and we'll handle the rest.

          Which payment methods do you accept?+

          UPI, PayPal, and major debit/credit cards — whichever is most convenient for you, from anywhere in the world.

          Will the file work on my device?+

          Yes. We deliver in PDF, EPUB, or MOBI — compatible with Kindle, iPad, Kobo, Android, and any e-reader or desktop app. Just tell us your preferred format.

          Why use Kitab Guru instead of free PDF sites?+

          Free sites can work, but they often mean broken files, missing pages, wrong editions, and wasted time hunting for something that actually opens. With Kitab Guru you get the exact edition you need, in the format you want, checked and sent by a real person. And when a title is genuinely hard to find, we do the sourcing for you. You’re paying for reliability, the right version, and your time back.

          What if the file quality isn't good?+

          We’re upfront before you ever pay. If a file is a scanned copy or anything less than clean, readable quality, we tell you the exact status first so you can decide with full clarity. We’d rather keep things transparent than surprise you. If you receive a file and something genuinely affects the reading experience, just send us a screenshot or a note, and we’ll look into it right away.

          What's your refund policy?+

          If a delivered file has a real issue — major missing pages or something that clearly affects readability — let us know with a quick screenshot or detail. We verify it, and if it checks out, we send you a better copy where one is available. If we can’t source a clean replacement, you get a full 100% refund. No hassle, no back-and-forth. We’re available 24/7, before and after your order, and your satisfaction is what we’re here for.

          I lost my file — can I get it resent for free?+

          Yes, within 7 days of delivery — just message us and we'll resend it at no cost. After 7 days, a re-send isn't free, since it means re-sourcing and re-verifying the file on our end. We'll quote you a reduced price at that time (typically 50–80% of what you originally paid, depending on the title), and always confirm it with you before charging anything.
